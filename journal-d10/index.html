Why this matters

Clarity is power. When you know exactly what you want, and what you will never accept, you start to notice it, and you start to become the person who attracts it. Write fast, be honest, and be passionate, like a kid making a list for Santa.

Tape homework: do the exercises, not just listen. Stop the audio when Tony says to.

1

My mate from heaven: the ultimate relationship, not what I would settle for.

"Whether I'm in a relationship or not, describe the ultimate relationship." Write as much detail as you can, as fast as you can, for 3 to 5 minutes. Stop the tape and do it now. Passion matters: this is a Santa list, and you know it is going to be delivered.

2

My musts: the things I cannot live without.

"Many of these are shoulds. Which are the musts?" Go back to your heaven list and put an asterisk (*) next to every quality you truly cannot live without. Then copy just those here. Musts, not preferences.

3

The person I could never be with (the "mate from hell"), then flip it.

"You also have to be absolutely clear about what you don't want." Describe this person in every detail: emotionally, spiritually, physically, how they connect with people, how they communicate, how they deal with life. It is okay to have fun with it. Then star the absolute NEVERs, the few that would destroy a relationship. The opposite of this list is your mate from heaven.

4

Who I must BECOME to attract all of that.

"You need to become the kind of person you want to find." Write a paragraph: what kind of person would it take? Your values, your character traits, how you conduct yourself, and what you do with your body, mind, emotions, and spirit. Tape examples: "I need to come from unconditional love, be playful, take care of myself, keep growing, and live in gratitude." / "Honest, creative, funny, kind, an open communicator, driven by values."

5

Where am I really? Score 0 to 10.

"To close the gap between where you are and where you want to be, you first have to know where you really are." Star the musts from your "who I must become" paragraph and list them below. Score YOURSELF as if the person you want were scoring you, not "yeah baby, I'm the best." 10 = fully, 0 = not at all. No partner needed: you are scoring yourself alone.

6

The 4 R's that quietly destroy relationships.

From Dr. John Gray and Dr. Barbara De Angelis: Resistance (something bugs you and you say nothing), Resentment (it builds and the tone changes), Rejection (you pick at them or pull away), Repression (you go numb, comfortable but no spark). Stacking is the trap: you end up reacting to everything at once. The fix: communicate honestly, early.

7

Action plan: how am I going to attract this person?

"You can't just sit around and hope they're going to show up. This is marketing." Proximity is power: put yourself where the kind of person you described spends time, on a regular basis. Be the person on your list, and love yourself first so there is already happiness inside you.

8

My magic moments.

"Make a list of magic moments, little ones and big ones: the laugh, the smile, the first time you met." What you capture, you anchor. List the magic moments with the people you love, and what you are grateful for in your connections.

9

Time together: what special things will I schedule this month?

The tape's time rules, for any relationship you care about: something special at least every 10 days; at least 3 special moments a month; a getaway every 3 to 6 months; and twice a year, a recap of your magic moments plus the goals for the next stretch, with dates.

10

Deepening my relationship with myself and my Creator.

"With a strong relationship with your Creator and a deep relationship with yourself, you have the foundation for anyone else." In your own words, in your own way: what do you want to ask for, and what would deepening that connection look like this week?
